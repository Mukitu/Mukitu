## 👋 About Me

I'm **Nishat**, a Computer Science & Engineering student who builds **production-grade software across three disciplines** — full-stack web, cross-platform mobile, and applied AI/ML. I'm the **Founder & Owner of [SkillProof](https://skillproof.top)**, a skill-verification and roadmap-based learning platform, where I design the product, build the backend, and structure the entire curriculum engine myself.

I don't just write code — I **architect, ship, and own products end-to-end**, from idea to a live SaaS with real users.

- 🏢 Founder & Owner — **[SkillProof](https://skillproof.top)** (skill verification & structured learning roadmaps)
- 💻 Full Stack Developer — **MERN** (MongoDB, Express, React, Node.js)
- 📱 Mobile Developer — **Flutter** (cross-platform Android/iOS apps)
- 🤖 AI/ML Engineer — building AI-powered products, not just prototypes
- 🎓 B.Sc in CSE, North Bengal International University
- 🌏 Deep, native-level understanding of the **Bangladeshi tech & F-commerce ecosystem**
